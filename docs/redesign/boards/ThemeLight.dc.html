Rules for light

Primary action is black with lime text. A lime button on paper has no visible edge.

Ratings and "Following" use olive, never lime. Active tabs and "Near you" stay black, as in dark mode they stay white.

Picked timetable blocks keep a lime tint behind black text, with an olive border.

Raver passport stays as is in both themes: it is already a light card.
